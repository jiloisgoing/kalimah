Lock Screen

How do I put the quote and moon on my Lock Screen?

Easiest way, from the Settings app:

  1. Open the Settings app and tap Wallpaper.
  2. Under the Lock Screen picture on the left, tap Customize.
  3. Tap the box under the clock.
  4. Find Quranscroll and tap Quote & moon. You can also add Moon or Next prayer.
  5. Tap Done.

Or from the Lock Screen: let Face ID unlock your iPhone but stay on the Lock Screen, then touch and hold an empty spot and tap Customize. If holding does nothing, your iPhone is still locked.

The app shows these steps with pictures under Settings → Lock Screen, and tells you when the widget has been added.

How often does the quote change?

Every 15 minutes by default. You can change it to every 30 minutes, every hour or once a day in Quranscroll under Settings → Lock Screen → New quote.

Can the Lock Screen widget be in color?

iOS draws all Lock Screen widgets in one color that matches your clock, so no app can color them. For changing colors, open Settings → Lock Screen in Quranscroll and tap Save 6 wallpapers to Photos. Then in the Settings app go to Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photo Shuffle, pick the six wallpapers, set the frequency to Hourly, and add the Quote & moon widget. The Home Screen widget changes color by itself with each new quote.

Why does the widget show a sun instead of the moon?

The moon appears from Maghrib until Fajr, drawn in that night's real phase. During the day you'll see a sun.

Prayer times and reminders

My prayer times are different from my mosque's.

Mosques use different calculation methods. In Quranscroll go to Settings → Prayer times and try a different Calculation method (for example ISNA in North America, Karachi in South Asia, Umm al-Qura in Saudi Arabia). If you follow the Hanafi school, set Asr time to Hanafi. Also check that Location is correct.

I'm not getting prayer reminders.
  1. In Quranscroll, go to Settings → Prayer reminders and turn on the prayers you want.
  2. In the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Quranscroll, make sure notifications are allowed.
  3. If a Focus mode (like Sleep or Do Not Disturb) is on, allow Quranscroll in that Focus.
  4. Open Quranscroll at least once a week. Reminders are scheduled 7 days ahead and refresh each time you open the app.
The Qibla direction doesn't look right.

Hold your phone flat, away from metal, magnets and electronics. If the app says accuracy is low, move your phone in a figure 8 a few times to calibrate the compass. Make sure your location is set correctly under Settings → Location.

Quran

Recitation won't play.

Reading the Quran works offline, but recitation (by Mishary Rashid Alafasy) streams over the internet. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again. Recitation keeps playing when your screen is locked, and you can control it from the Lock Screen.

How do I make the Arabic bigger or hide the translation?

In any surah, tap the Aa button at the top right for larger or smaller text, and to show or hide the translation.

Language

How do I change the app's language?

Quranscroll is available in English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian and Turkish. It follows your iPhone's language. To choose a different language just for Quranscroll, go to the iPhone Settings app → Quranscroll → Language, or in Quranscroll tap Settings → About → Language.

If you don't see a Language option, first add the language under Settings → General → Language & Region.

Content

Where do the verses and hadith come from?

Quran text comes from the Tanzil Project. The English translation is Saheeh International, Urdu is by Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry, Indonesian is by the Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs, and Turkish is by Diyanet. Hadith come only from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, numbered as on sunnah.com so you can check each one. The full list is in the app under Settings → About → Our sources.

Do I need an account? Is my data collected?

No account is needed, and Quranscroll has no ads or tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
