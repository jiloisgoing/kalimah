Kalimah

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version: Kalimah has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else. Everything stays on your phone.

Quran recitation

When you press play on a recitation, the audio streams from the Islamic Network audio server (cdn.islamic.network). Like any website, that server sees your device's IP address so it can send the audio. Kalimah sends it nothing else: no account, no identifiers, no information about you. Reading the Quran works offline and makes no connection.

Your location

If you allow it, Kalimah uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This happens on your phone. Your location is never sent anywhere. You can choose a city instead, or turn location off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. No server is involved, and we can't see which reminders you turn on.

What's stored on your phone

Your settings, saved quotes, tasbih count and the location used for prayer times are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Kalimah doesn't use iCloud or any server to store your data.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets read the same on-device settings to show prayer times and quotes. They don't connect to the internet.

Sharing

When you share or copy a quote, it goes only where you send it, using the standard iOS share sheet.

Third parties

Kalimah contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Prayer times are calculated with the open-source Adhan library, which runs on your phone and sends nothing.

Children

Kalimah doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here and in the app, with a new date at the top.
